# What Blueprint plays, and how we tested responses

We tested Dushyant Betala's public [Blueprint revision ba64c26](https://github.com/Dushyant-Betala/heads-up-bundle/tree/ba64c267aa2e8c442e5e5f824539c400faea7a64). The inference model in this revision is a group of precomputed probability tables. It does not run a neural network to choose a bet.

| Part | What the published implementation does |
|---|---|
| Training | The author reports **68 million deals** of external-sampling Monte Carlo counterfactual regret minimization (MCCFR). It samples opponent actions, compares the available actions at a training player's information state, and averages learned strategies. [Author's release note](https://github.com/Dushyant-Betala/heads-up-bundle/commit/ba64c267aa2e8c442e5e5f824539c400faea7a64) · [Trainer](https://github.com/Dushyant-Betala/heads-up-bundle/blob/ba64c267aa2e8c442e5e5f824539c400faea7a64/training/engine.py#L473-L539) |
| Hand representation | Five hole cards plus the visible board map to **40 preflop, 64 flop, 64 turn, or 14 river buckets**. Flop and turn features include the average hand strength and the frequency of strong future outcomes. [Hand abstraction](https://github.com/Dushyant-Betala/heads-up-bundle/blob/ba64c267aa2e8c442e5e5f824539c400faea7a64/training/build_abstraction.py#L80-L94) |
| Betting representation | The lookup distinguishes position, current betting sequence, prior aggressor, pot size and board texture. Each table allocates **2,335,168 rows**, each with four action weights (fold, check/call, half-pot, pot). Allocated rows are not the same as visited training states. It filters illegal actions and samples from the remaining weights. [Constants](https://github.com/Dushyant-Betala/heads-up-bundle/blob/ba64c267aa2e8c442e5e5f824539c400faea7a64/brain.py#L29-L48) · [State key](https://github.com/Dushyant-Betala/heads-up-bundle/blob/ba64c267aa2e8c442e5e5f824539c400faea7a64/brain.py#L334-L344) |
| Adaptation | It has one base strategy and four responses (nit, tight-aggressive, fit-or-fold, and pot-odds). It scores observed bets against 11 profiles. It requires at least **120 opponent actions** and adequate statistical fit to switch. A loss limit can revert the switch. [Profile and action selection](https://github.com/Dushyant-Betala/heads-up-bundle/blob/ba64c267aa2e8c442e5e5f824539c400faea7a64/brain.py#L429-L507) |

Our response learns expected chip returns against the published strategy in **offline simulation**. At play time it sees only its hole cards, the public board and betting history allowed by the referee. The GPU-trained tables retain the order and size of preflop bets and pressure from the previous street. Another specialist groups hands by their distribution of future strength so it can distinguish two hands with the same average equity. Which of these details helps is determined by timed matches against the actual executable.

An early gate detects evidence that the opponent often opens for half pot but reraises for full pot. It uses observable action counts, not an opponent name. Our two additional 2,000-hand trace matches against Weights 2 showed net gains in pots that ended with a fold and losses in pots that went to showdown. The breakdown is descriptive: changing a policy also changes which hands reach showdown. [Trace accounting](../two-opponents-20261107/blueprint-extra-traces.json)

The goal for the two named rivals is zero recorded losses, positive chips, and outright wins on fresh match seeds. The public 25-team proxy and the official contest leaderboard are separate. The supplied leaderboard is not evidence that the public repository and the contest submission are identical.
